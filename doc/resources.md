## Judging

**Prize tracks**

Live London

Make London the best city to live

Improve everyday life in London: transport, housing, civic life, local business, accessibility, safety, affordability, loneliness, bureaucracy. Or help people make the most of what London already offers: neighbourhoods, culture, events, food, communities, third spaces.

Build London

Make London the best city to build

Strengthen London’s builder ecosystem: make it easier to start, launch, grow and create here, and easier to navigate London’s startup, AI, creative, research and finance scenes.

**What we look for**

Idea validation

Evidence the problem is real and worth solving for Londoners.

Technical approach

How well the build is engineered and uses the stack.

Project readiness

How complete and demoable it is on the day.

UX / design

How clear, considered and nice to use it feels.

## What Londoners are asking for

Real, validated requests from Londoners, and the people you'd be helping. Optional: build for one of these, or bring your own.

Finding your people in a new city

A free evening when staying in and scrolling feels like the easy choice.

**Who you'd help:** Newcomers and anyone whose default is to stay home alone.

Leaving an event having met no one who matters, or starting a project with no team.

**Who you'd help:** Founders, builders, designers and operators trying to find the right collaborators in a fragmented London scene.

Moving through London

A wheelchair user or parent with a buggy mid-journey, when a lift fails and the original plan ends.

**Who you'd help:** Wheelchair users, parents with buggies, anyone who can't take stairs.

Committing to a place to live

About to sign a tenancy on a street you know nothing about.

**Who you'd help:** First-time renters and anyone moving to an unfamiliar London postcode.

Choosing between a flat closer to work vs cheaper rent further out.

**Who you'd help:** Renters weighing rent, commute, hours and money.

**Real-world examples:** [Moving-to-London dashboard](https://x.com/ShanuMathew93/status/2005308532871045283) · [Renter's passport (tenant_register)](https://x.com/drmillybarker/status/2070474113638875228)

Discovering the city beyond your patch

A free hour with phone in hand, defaulting to the same five places.

**Who you'd help:** Residents and newcomers who only walk the same three streets between home, work and the tube.

An ordinary walk past extraordinary history nobody tells you about.

**Who you'd help:** Anyone walking through London willing to be surprised.

Getting a venture off the ground

Deciding to start something real-world and hitting the wall of 'what am I legally required to do?'

**Who you'd help:** First-time founders and small-business starters (e.g. food stall, short-let, childminding service, clinic).

Every time a team re-scrapes the same tired sources because what Londoners know never becomes callable.

**Who you'd help:** The whole London builder ecosystem: the people building everything else on this list.

**Real-world examples:** [Xylo (AI for planning, £2.8m pre-seed)](https://www.buildxylo.ai/) · [ukgovscan](https://ukgovscan.com) · [wheredoesitallgo](https://wheredoesitallgo.org) (already built, build on these)

**The planning bottleneck:** [air-con needs planning permission (up to 8 weeks)](https://www.chroniclelive.co.uk/news/uk-news/uk-rule-means-air-con-34177148) · [planning is slow and arbitrary](https://x.com/Minuetter/status/2022325900080013751)

Getting help from public services & care

When someone entitled to support can't navigate the website to claim it.

**Who you'd help:** Elderly, low-literacy and non-English-speaking Londoners blocked by dense forms.

Someone eligible for support (benefits, refunds, hardship funds, bursaries, dormant accounts) and never claiming it.

**Who you'd help:** ONE specific group (e.g. low-income parents, recent migrants, students, carers) who routinely miss out on support they're owed.

Building a future here: work & learning

Applying endlessly with no experience and no way into a shifting tech/AI job market.

**Who you'd help:** Young adults, career-switchers and AI-displaced workers in ONE specific sector.

Walking into a GP appointment, a job interview, or a parents' evening, knowing the conversation will be hard.

**Who you'd help:** Non-native English speakers wanting to handle one specific real-life situation with confidence.
